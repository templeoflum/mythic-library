E. T. Whittaker, "On the Functions which are represented by the Expansions of the Interpolation-Theory," *Proceedings of the Royal Society of Edinburgh* 35 (1915), 181–194. [Publisher / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0370164600017806). [Primary scan](https://zenodo.org/records/1428702/files/article.pdf).

**source claim** — Section 1 considers functions agreeing on equally spaced arguments; Sections 4–6 construct and analyze the cardinal function and its interpolation series. Section 8 summarizes its relation to the sampled values and the absence of shorter-period constituents. These are claims of the historical paper, not an assertion that every analytic function is determined by its samples.

**library role (editorial)** — supplies the missing primary citation for the 1915 interpolation lineage already described in [[object.sampling-theorem]]. It concerns interpolation theory rather than a communication-channel theorem. Modern convergence and function-space qualifications should be checked separately.

**identity caution** — E. T. Whittaker is distinct from J. M. Whittaker, whose 1935 book is cited in [[source.shannon-1949]].

**verification** — author/title and received/read dates appear on p. 181; the publisher confirms volume, year, pages, and DOI. Checked 2026-10-14. The publisher's 2014 online date is not the original publication year.
