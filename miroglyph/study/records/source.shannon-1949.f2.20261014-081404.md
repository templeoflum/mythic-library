C. E. Shannon, "Communication in the Presence of Noise," *Proceedings of the IRE* 37(1) (January 1949), 10–21. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/JRPROC.1949.232969).

**primary-text check** — Section II contains **Theorem 1**, the sampling result, and equation (7), its sinc reconstruction. Notes 3–6 cite J. M. Whittaker's *Interpolatory Function Theory* (1935), Nyquist, Bennett, and Gabor. The Whittaker citation here is distinct from E. T. Whittaker's 1915 paper [[source.whittaker-1915]].

Section VII, Theorem 2, gives C = W log₂(1 + P/N) bits per second for the stated additive white thermal-noise model, with average signal power P and noise power N in bandwidth W. The noise assumptions and logarithm base matter.
